Monthly plans

Investing a fixed amount every month through your bank, like POSB Invest-Saver or OCBC's Blue Chip Investment Plan? Add the plan once and SGXray records each month's buy for you: on your buy day (or the next trading day), at that day's closing price, in whole shares after the fee, like the banks do. Your bank's own price can differ by a few cents; change any month's trade to match your statement.
