The ground rules

Using SGXray means you agree to these. They're short, and in plain English on purpose.

Education, not advice

SGXray explains numbers and teaches the basics. It doesn't tell you what to buy, sell or hold, and nothing on it is a recommendation or financial advice. SGXray isn't a licensed financial adviser and isn't regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. Every decision about your money is yours: if you need advice, speak to a licensed financial adviser. Past returns don't predict future ones, and investing can lose money.

The numbers are estimates

Prices and dividends come from Yahoo Finance as end-of-day closes, not live prices, and can be late, missing or wrong. Your figures are only as accurate as the trades you enter. Forecasts assume the past repeats, which it often doesn't. For anything that matters (taxes, legal records, selling), use your broker's and CDP's own statements. The practice portfolio uses pretend money: no real trade ever happens on SGXray.

Your part

  • Don't try to break in, overload the site, get at other people's data, scrape it or create accounts automatically. Found a security problem? Report it privately (see the security log), and thank you.
  • Only enter your own data.

Accounts that break these rules may be suspended or deleted.
