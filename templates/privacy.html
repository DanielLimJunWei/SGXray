Privacy notice · last changed 25 November 2026

What SGXray keeps, and why

SGXray is a learning and portfolio-tracking site for people investing in Singapore. It keeps as little about you as it can, never sells it, and shows no ads. This page follows Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What it keeps

Your email address
To log you in, and to send a password-reset link if you ask for one. Nothing else: no newsletters, no marketing.
Your password
Never the password itself: only a scrambled, salted version (a scrypt hash) that can check it but can't be turned back into it.
Your trades, notes and monthly plans
What you type in, to work out your portfolio. Your practice portfolio is kept the same way, apart from your real one.
Lessons you've finished
Only which ones and when, so Learn can show your progress. Not how you answered anything.
Security records
When you sign up, log in or out, change your password or your trades, with the network address it came from: kept 180 days, to spot and stop break-ins. Failed log-in counts are kept one day at most.
A login cookie
One cookie, holding a random session number, so you stay logged in for up to 7 days. It's needed for the site to work. There are no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

Not kept: your answers to "Am I ready to start?" (they're used once to show the result, then gone), your broker or bank details (SGXray never asks for them), and anything about you from other sites.

In your browser only (never sent to SGXray): whether animations are on, Simple or Detailed view, and, when you're logged out, which lessons you've read. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Who else is involved

  • The hosting company that runs SGXray's server and database stores everything above on SGXray's behalf, under contract, and may not use it for anything else.
  • An email service receives your email address when a password-reset email is sent.
  • Yahoo Finance supplies prices and dividends. SGXray's server asks it about stock codes only: never anything about you.
  • News publishers (CNA, The Business Times, The Straits Times, The Edge Singapore): SGXray's server reads their public headline feeds. Your browser only contacts them if you open an article.
  • jsDelivr, a code library service, supplies the home page's 3D scene, so your browser contacts it when you open the home page.

Your data isn't sold, rented or shared with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

What you can do

  • See and download it: Account → Download my data gives you everything above in one file.
  • Correct it: change or delete any trade, note or plan on your Trades page, and your password on your Account page.
  • Delete it: Account → Delete my account removes your account and everything in it straight away. Server backups that may still hold it are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Ask or complain: {% if contact %}email {{ contact }}, SGXray's data protection contact, and you'll get an answer within 30 days{% else %}contact SGXray's data protection officer (the address is added here before launch){% endif %}. You can also contact Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission at pdpc.gov.sg.

Keeping it safe

Pages are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Every form is protected against forgery, nobody can open another person's portfolio, and log-ins slow right down after a few wrong passwords. How each protection works and is tested is published in SGXray's security log. If a data breach ever puts you at risk, you'll be told, and so will the PDPC within 3 days, as the PDPA requires.

SGXray is meant for adults. If you're under 18, use it with a parent's or guardian's agreement.
